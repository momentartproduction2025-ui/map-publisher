MAP Publisher · Effective 6 October 2026

1. What MAP Publisher is

MAP Publisher is an internal tool operated by Moment Art Production (Jeddah) to manage its own social media accounts. It runs on the operator's own computer. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and has no users other than the operator and people the operator personally authorises.

2. Who may use it, and for which accounts

Only the operator and people the operator authorises may use it. It may be connected only to accounts that the operator owns or is authorised to manage. Connecting an account is done through that platform's official authorisation page, and the account holder can withdraw it at any time (see section 6).

3. What it does

  • Connects to an account through the platform's official sign-in and confirms which account it is (username and display name).
  • Reads account information the account holder authorised: for TikTok, basic profile, profile details, account statistics and the list of public videos.
  • Can prepare content and, where the platform allows it, create a draft or post it. This is switched off by default and needs a separate, named human approval for every single action. Nothing is posted, uploaded, edited or deleted automatically.
  • As of the effective date above, MAP Publisher has not posted any content to TikTok.

4. What it does not do

  • It does not post, comment, reply or send messages unattended, and it does not send messages to people on TikTok.
  • It does not sell or share account data, run advertising, or collect data about visitors.
  • It does not work around platform limits. For example, TikTok restricts content posted by apps that TikTok has not audited to private visibility, and MAP Publisher does not try to change that.

5. Platform rules

Use of each connected platform stays subject to that platform's own terms and policies, including TikTok's Terms of Service and TikTok's developer terms. The operator is responsible for the content that is approved for publishing and for having the rights to it.

6. Disconnecting

The operator can disconnect a TikTok account at any time with the tool's disconnect command, which asks TikTok to revoke the access and deletes the stored credential, or by removing the app's access in the TikTok account's own app-permission settings. What is kept and what is deleted is described in the Privacy Policy.

7. Availability and changes

This is a private tool provided without any promise of availability, and it may change or be discontinued at any time. These terms may be updated; the effective date above shows the current version.
