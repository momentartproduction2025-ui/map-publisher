MAP Publisher

Operated by Moment Art Production · Updated 6 October 2026

MAP Publisher is a private tool that Moment Art Production uses to manage its own social media accounts (and the accounts of its team lead, Tamer Samih) from one place: checking which account is connected, reading account information, and preparing content for publishing.

What it is, and what it is not

  • It runs as a program on the operator's own computer. It is not a website or app that members of the public sign up for or use.
  • There are no user accounts, no visitor tracking, and no data is collected from people who read these pages.
  • It only connects accounts that the operator owns or is authorised to manage, through each platform's official sign-in (for TikTok, TikTok Login Kit).
  • Anything that would post, upload or change something on a platform is switched off by default and needs a separate, named approval from a person for each action. Nothing posts on its own.

TikTok

MAP Publisher uses TikTok Login Kit (desktop flow) so the operator can authorise it for a TikTok account the operator controls. It asks only for the permissions it needs: basic profile information, profile details, account statistics and the list of public videos, plus permission to create drafts or post videos, which is not used without an approval for each action. The details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
